Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in CodeArena Pro?

Pro unlocks all 50 advanced, curated DSA challenges covering complex graphs, segment trees, interval DP, and multi-tier progressive hints that guide your intuition without giving away the answer.

How does Direct UPI payment verification work?

Initiate an upgrade to generate your order reference and dynamic QR code. Pay using any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), then submit the 12-digit UPI reference / UTR number. Our administrators verify the transaction in our bank statement and unlock Pro on your account.

Are the 200 free challenges always free?

Yes. All 50 Basic Java challenges and 150 foundational DSA challenges remain 100% free forever for all registered students and guests.

How long does verification take?

Administrator verification is completed promptly once the UTR is submitted. You can check the current status of your order anytime on the Upgrade page or Dashboard.
